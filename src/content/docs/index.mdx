## Who We Are

We are a group of Canadian meshes across the country from that are working together to bring services and standards across Canada for all MeshCore users. We host MQTT servers and a packet analyzer at this site for all Canadians to use.
The servers and services are currently managed by [**MrAlders0n**](https://github.com/MrAlders0n), [**Ded**](https://github.com/446564), and [**n30nex**](https://github.com/n30nex).

## Want to Contribute?

This site is community-driven. If you want to add your local mesh, fix a typo, or contribute a build guide, check out the [Contributing](contributing) page.
